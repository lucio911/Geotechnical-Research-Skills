# Reviewer Rubric

Score each dimension 0–4, but do not average away fatal flaws.

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem significance | trivial/unclear | useful but narrow | important and clearly framed |
| Novelty | duplicated/cosmetic | incremental evidence | convincing new mechanism/method/capability |
| Mechanics | inconsistent | plausible but incomplete | coherent and well tested |
| Method reproducibility | insufficient | mostly recoverable | fully auditable |
| Evidence strength | descriptive | quantitative | triangulated/validated |
| Uncertainty/robustness | absent | partial | appropriate and convincing |
| Claim discipline | overclaimed | mostly bounded | precise evidence-matched claims |
| Transferability | unjustified | bounded | validated across relevant conditions |
| Figure architecture | decorative/redundant | serviceable | evidence-efficient |
| Manuscript spine | fragmented | understandable | strong central argument |

### Decision rule

A score is diagnostic only. Any Fatal finding overrides aggregate score.
