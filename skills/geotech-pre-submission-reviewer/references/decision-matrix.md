# Editorial Decision Matrix

## Ready
No fatal/major scientific issue. Remaining changes are presentation-level.

## Minor repair
Core claims are defensible. A few bounded clarifications, consistency fixes, or small analyses are needed.

## Major repair
Central contribution may be publishable, but at least one major claim lacks required evidence, validation, novelty differentiation, or structural coherence.

## Not yet defensible
One or more fatal issues undermine the central contribution and require new evidence/model redesign/theory repair rather than manuscript editing.

## Minimum revision package

For each proposed revision ask:

1. Which exact reviewer finding does it close?
2. Which claim becomes defensible afterward?
3. Is this the smallest action that resolves the risk?
4. Does it require new data, new analysis, new theory, or only rewriting?

Avoid long wish lists unrelated to rejection risk.
