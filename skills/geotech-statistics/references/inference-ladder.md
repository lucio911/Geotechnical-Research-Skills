# Statistical Inference Ladder

1. **Description** — what the observed dataset shows.
2. **Uncertainty** — precision/variability around a quantity.
3. **Contrast** — difference between predefined conditions.
4. **Association** — covariation after accounting for design/dependence.
5. **Prediction** — out-of-sample predictive performance.
6. **Mechanism** — requires design/mechanical discrimination beyond statistical association.

Do not skip from Level 3/4 to Level 6 because a coefficient is significant.
