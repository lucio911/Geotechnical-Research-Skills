# Claim Record — v0.4

Recommended fields:

- `claim_id`
- `exact_wording`
- `claim_level`
- `claim_type`
- `status`
- `manuscript_locations`
- `direct_evidence_ids`
- `corroborating_evidence_ids`
- `mechanism_ids`
- `contradictory_evidence_ids`
- `boundary_ids`
- `literature_source_ids`
- `strongest_evidence_grade`
- `alternative_explanations`
- `required_downgrade_if_evidence_missing`
- `confidence`

The exact wording is important because "is associated with", "contributes to", and "governs" are different claims even when they concern the same variables.
