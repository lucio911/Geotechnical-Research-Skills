# Common Geotechnical Unit Traps

Check for:

- Pa/kPa/MPa mixing;
- N/kN mixing;
- mm/m conversion;
- density versus unit weight;
- mass density versus dry density;
- total versus effective stress;
- engineering versus true strain where relevant;
- decimal strain versus percent strain;
- displacement versus normalized displacement;
- radians versus degrees;
- frequency Hz versus angular frequency rad/s;
- pore pressure sign conventions;
- compression-positive versus tension-positive conventions;
- diameter/radius confusion;
- model-scale versus prototype-scale units.
