# Evidence Grades

## E0 — descriptive
Single visual pattern or anecdotal observation; no robust quantification.

## E1 — quantified
One defined metric with traceable magnitude/trend.

## E2 — robustness checked
Replication, uncertainty, sensitivity, or repeated trend supports E1.

## E3 — triangulated
Independent response variables or methods converge on the same interpretation.

## E4 — discriminating/validated
Evidence distinguishes the proposed mechanism from credible alternatives or validates prediction independently.

## E5 — generalized
Independent materials, stress paths, geometries, scales, or datasets reproduce the core relation.

### Rule

A high R² is not automatically a high evidence grade. Fit quality does not replace independent validation, mechanism discrimination, or uncertainty analysis.
