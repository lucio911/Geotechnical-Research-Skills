# Figure Role Map

Use one row per main figure.

| Figure | Question answered | Evidence type | Claim IDs | Unique role | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fig. X | What does it resolve? | validation/trend/mechanism/boundary | SC-01 | why another figure cannot replace it | keep/combine/move/delete |

Useful role labels:

- `V`: validity/verification;
- `P`: primary phenomenon;
- `C`: controlling factor;
- `M`: mechanism discrimination;
- `B`: boundary/transition;
- `T`: theory validation;
- `S`: synthesis.

Contour plots are usually explanatory/spatial evidence unless quantitatively extracted.
