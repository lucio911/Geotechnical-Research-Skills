# Section Budget

Allocate manuscript space by scientific importance, not by amount of work performed.

A useful default logic:

- Introduction: only context needed to establish the gap.
- Methods: enough detail for interpretation and reproducibility.
- Results: most space to evidence supporting MC and SC claims.
- Discussion: mechanism, comparison, alternatives, limits.
- Conclusions: compact claim compression.

A subsection should answer a scientific question. Avoid headings that are merely parameter labels such as "Effect of 100 kPa", "Effect of 120 kPa", etc., unless the staged loading itself is the scientific variable.
