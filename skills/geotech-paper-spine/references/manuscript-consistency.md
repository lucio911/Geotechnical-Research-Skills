# Manuscript Consistency Audit

Check every major claim across these surfaces:

| Surface | Check |
|---|---|
| Title | Does it promise only what is demonstrated? |
| Abstract | Are objective, methods, results, and conclusion aligned? |
| Introduction | Is the stated gap actually addressed? |
| Methods | Can the design resolve the gap? |
| Results | Is direct evidence shown? |
| Discussion | Is mechanism distinguished from observation? |
| Captions | Do captions describe evidence without overclaiming? |
| Conclusions | Are all statements traceable to prior evidence? |

Flag wording drift: a claim that changes from "associated with" in Results to "governs" in Conclusions without additional evidence.
