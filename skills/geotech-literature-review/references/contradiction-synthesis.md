# Contradiction Synthesis

When two studies disagree, do not average their conclusions.

Check in this order:

1. Are response variables defined identically?
2. Are stress measures and reference states comparable?
3. Are drainage and hydraulic conditions comparable?
4. Are soil/rock state and fabric comparable?
5. Is the loading path, amplitude, rate, or cycle count comparable?
6. Are scale and boundary conditions comparable?
7. Is one conclusion experimentally observed while the other is numerically inferred?
8. Could measurement or constitutive assumptions explain the divergence?

Classify disagreement as definitional, boundary-condition dependent, material-state dependent, method dependent, statistically unresolved, or genuine unresolved scientific contradiction.

Preserve genuine disagreement rather than forcing consensus.
