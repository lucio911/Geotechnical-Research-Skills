# Sensor QC checklist

For each channel inspect:

- calibration equation and validity range;
- resolution/noise floor;
- overload/saturation;
- zero drift;
- hysteresis/backlash where relevant;
- temperature/environment sensitivity;
- sampling/anti-alias settings;
- sign/orientation;
- synchronization with controller channels.

A response smaller than measurement uncertainty/resolution cannot support a strong trend claim.
