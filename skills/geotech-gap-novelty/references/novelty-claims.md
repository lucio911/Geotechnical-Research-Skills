# Novelty Claim Patterns

## Avoid

- "For the first time" unless exhaustive verification is possible.
- "Few studies have..." as the entire gap.
- "A novel model is proposed" when only coefficient fitting changed.
- "Systematically investigated" as a substitute for contribution.

## Prefer structure

`Prior capability -> unresolved limitation -> why that limitation matters -> what this study uniquely measures/tests/derives -> evidence that closes the gap -> scope`

## Example skeleton

> Previous studies establish **A** under **conditions B**, but cannot determine **C** because **evidence/method limitation D**. This study therefore tests **hypothesis E** by jointly measuring/deriving **F and G**. The resulting evidence identifies **H** within **scope I**, providing **mechanistic/predictive/engineering contribution J**.

The actual wording must remain no stronger than the evidence.
