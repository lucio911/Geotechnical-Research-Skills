# Common Geotechnical Confounders

Audit at minimum:

- initial void ratio/relative density;
- water content, degree of saturation, suction;
- consolidation stress and OCR;
- effective versus total stress;
- fabric/specimen preparation;
- particle size relative to apparatus dimensions;
- membrane/interface effects;
- drainage length and loading rate;
- stress-path sequence;
- previous cyclic damage or particle breakage;
- temperature for rate-sensitive materials;
- sensor zero drift and seating effects;
- chamber/tank boundaries;
- pile/tunnel roughness and interface preparation;
- installation method;
- sample disturbance;
- curing/age for cemented or treated soils.

Treat confounders as design variables or explicit limitations, not as after-the-fact prose.
