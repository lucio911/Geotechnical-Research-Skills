# Similarity and Scale-Effect Audit

For physical model studies ask:

1. Which dimensionless groups govern the target mechanism?
2. Which similitude conditions are satisfied, approximated, or violated?
3. Does self-weight stress scale correctly?
4. Is particle size small relative to structural dimensions and shear-band thickness?
5. Are interface roughness and stiffness scale-dependent?
6. Are drainage and inertial time scales preserved?
7. Does boundary distance remain sufficiently remote?
8. Which outputs can be compared dimensionlessly?
9. Which conclusions are limited to model scale?

Do not infer field-scale capacity or failure thresholds from a small-scale model without a defensible scaling argument.
