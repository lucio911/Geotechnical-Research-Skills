# Cross-Paper Comparison Matrix

Before comparing numerical values, harmonize definitions—not just units.

Recommended columns:

| Paper | Material/state | Effective stress | Drainage | Loading path | Geometry/scale | Metric definition | Result | Mechanism support | Validation | Transferability |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Incompatibility flags

Flag comparisons when papers use different:

- reference stiffness/modulus;
- cyclic amplitude definitions;
- strain measures;
- sign conventions;
- failure criteria;
- normalized depths/diameters;
- effective vs total stress;
- drained vs undrained paths;
- laboratory vs field scale;
- constitutive parameter definitions.

A valid synthesis may conclude that direct quantitative comparison is not defensible.
