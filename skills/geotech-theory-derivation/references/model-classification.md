# Model Classification

## Mechanics-derived
Core functional form follows governing mechanics with limited closure/calibration.

## Constitutive/state-variable
Defines state evolution and response with physically interpretable internal variables, subject to constitutive assumptions and calibration.

## Mechanism-informed semi-empirical
Functional form is empirical/semi-empirical but variables/constraints are motivated by a plausible mechanism.

## Empirical phenomenological
Designed primarily to reproduce observed trends; physical interpretation is secondary.

## Surrogate/statistical
Approximates input-output behavior without claiming direct physical law.

### Wording rule

Use the weakest classification that is unquestionably defensible. Calling a semi-empirical degradation equation "mechanism-based" requires evidence that the proposed internal variables are necessary and discriminative, not merely correlated with the response.
