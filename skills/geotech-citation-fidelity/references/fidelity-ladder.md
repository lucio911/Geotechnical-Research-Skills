# Citation Fidelity Ladder

- **F0 — Unverified**: source content has not been checked.
- **F1 — Topical relevance**: source concerns the topic but does not support the specific proposition.
- **F2 — Broad consistency**: source is directionally compatible, but differs materially in variable, condition, scale, method, or inference level.
- **F3 — Direct qualitative support**: source directly supports the qualitative proposition under sufficiently matched conditions.
- **F4 — Direct quantitative support**: source directly reports the relevant magnitude/relationship/result with suitable conditions.
- **F5 — Strong mechanistic/causal support**: source contains evidence capable of supporting the mechanism/causal statement at the claimed level, including alternatives/boundaries where required.

A manuscript claim should not exceed the support level available from its citation set.
