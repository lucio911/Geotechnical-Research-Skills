# Citation Mismatch Taxonomy

Use one or more labels:

- `TOPIC_ONLY`
- `WRONG_VARIABLE`
- `WRONG_DIRECTION`
- `WRONG_MAGNITUDE`
- `CONDITION_MISMATCH`
- `MATERIAL_MISMATCH`
- `STRESS_PATH_MISMATCH`
- `DRAINAGE_MISMATCH`
- `SCALE_MISMATCH`
- `METHOD_MISMATCH`
- `ASSOCIATION_TO_CAUSALITY`
- `MECHANISM_OVERREACH`
- `GENERALIZATION_OVERREACH`
- `SECONDARY_SOURCE_FOR_PRIMARY_CLAIM`
- `VERSION_MISMATCH`
- `SOURCE_CONTRADICTS_CLAIM`

Prefer weakening or splitting a claim over stretching a citation beyond its evidence.
